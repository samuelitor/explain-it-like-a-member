How to play

1. SpinThe wheel picks an explainer, a hidden term, a member persona, and sometimes a twist.
2. Explain60 seconds to explain the term to that member. Saying the term itself is not allowed.
3. GuessEveryone else shouts guesses. The person playing the member must ask one in-character question.
4. ScoreFirst correct guesser gets 2 points. The explainer gets 1 if it was guessed, plus the room's 1–5 vote.
Team modeTeams take turns. Only the explainer's team can guess during the protected time, then any team can steal. Points add up per team.
